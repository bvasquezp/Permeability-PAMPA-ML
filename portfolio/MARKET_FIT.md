# Market fit | Freelance positioning — October 2026

Positioning: **Python Data Automation | Excel / CSV | Scientific Data Quality**.

Job signals observed across Workana and Upwork include spreadsheet cleanup, structured data extraction, recurring reports, invoice/payment reconciliation, ETL tasks, and scientific dataset curation. This is a qualitative opportunity scan, **not** a statistically representative demand survey.

## Commercial offers

1. **CSV/Excel data cleanup.** Fixed input schema, deduplication, validation checks, rejected-record file and short handover documentation. Demonstration: `demo.py` handles **CSV input**; actual Excel ingestion requires project-specific implementation.
2. **Payment/invoice record matching.** Deterministic invoice-ID reconciliation, exceptions and summary counts. No inferred allocation or handling of production banking credentials.
3. **Automated Excel summaries.** Repeatable CSV-to-XLSX reporting, with quality checks and reproducible commands.
4. **Document extraction/report generation.** PDF/Word templates and structured extraction as a potential extension, not yet built.
5. **Research data curation and scientific ML.** Literature-derived tables, data provenance, RDKit descriptors, model validation, interpretability and limitations; the repository root demonstrates domain experience.
6. **ETL/dashboard implementation.** Database/API ingestion, structured reporting and monitoring after separate feasibility review.

## Suggested proposal structure

**Problem → scope → inputs → validation rules → deliverables → timeline → fixed-price milestone → acceptance criteria.**

Prefer small first milestones; avoid promising an unlimited number of files, poorly defined OCR accuracy, or guaranteed modeling performance. Price ranges must be quoted after reviewing the actual input and output examples.

## Ethical/publication boundaries

- Do not reuse AtomForge nonpublic code, infrastructure details, proprietary datasets or unpublished business information.
- Obtain permission for redistribution of third-party research/chemical databases and figures.
- Do not claim work has been performed for paying clients based on this synthetic example.
- Follow confidentiality, personal-data and licensing requirements before accepting a real dataset.
